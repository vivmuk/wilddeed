# WildDeed — Build Brief

**Self-authored, not interviewed.** Vivek's PRD (wilddeed-prd.md) + "make it incredibly beautiful, professional, trustworthy, people will buy information from it" serve as the interview. Autonomous run per user preference.

## What this is
A naturalist's background check for any piece of US land. Enter an address, get a 7-page AI-written wildlife dossier (species, counts, conservation status, cover art, PDF) in ~3 minutes, built from real GBIF/IUCN data.

## Who it's for
Recreational/hunting land brokers (primary), land buyers doing due diligence on $100k+ purchases (secondary).

## The one sentence the page must install
> "This land's wildlife record is a data-backed document, not the seller's ad copy."

## Visitor's next action
Order a dossier. One label everywhere: **Order a Dossier**.

## Grammar: Chaptered editorial
Chosen from the eight. Why: WildDeed's product is literally a printed dossier; the page should feel like reading a field report, not watching a film. Chaptered forbids the scrub hero, drift, magnetic CTA — all correct for a trust product that should read as substance. The other seven lost: filmic one-shot (marketing-film feel, wrong for due diligence), live surface (no live product surface yet, fake dashboards banned), continuous world (no journey geography), typographic poster (we have real plate art — the brand's actual aesthetic), gallery (not a range of options), split stage (not a two-sided argument... though buyer-vs-seller-claims is close; chaptered serves the report structure better), rhythmic cutlist (energy brand, opposite of trust).

## Structure (folio margin nav, title page hero, colophon close)
- Nav: no fixed bar. A folio in the left margin: chapter number + title, updating as chapters pass. Clickable.
- Hero: **title page.** Painted survey plat map low in frame, type on the bone paper ground. No media above the fold.
- Chapters hard-cut between grounds (bone → deep forest → slate night), no drift.
- Close: colophon/masthead plate, small type, CTA set as a line of running text.

## Signature move
**The field ledger.** A narrow fixed rail (right margin, desktop) styled as a naturalist's tally strip. As the visitor passes each real data claim in the chapters, the ledger stamps a real number from the Babcock WI sample report (records examined: 92,840; species: 518; eagle sightings: 42; crane status: endangered). By the colophon the ledger is a totaled record of what they just read, doubling as a mini data appendix. Real numbers only (from the actual generated report). Mobile: collapses into chapter-end totals inline.

## Feeling curve (written before acts)
1. Recognition — their own world: a Wisconsin hunting parcel at first light (ch1, land plate)
2. Unease — what they don't know before they sign (ch2, the problem, plain type)
3. Curiosity — what a dossier actually holds (ch3 spread, journal plates)
4. Awe — the whooping crane plate; endangered, on this land (PEAK, ch4, biggest act)
5. Trust — the method: real counts, real sources, honest limits (ch5, ledger fills)
6. Resolve — the colophon: order the record (ch6 close, small quiet type)

Peak: "the page went quiet and a whooping crane taller than the headline was standing on the land I was about to buy."

Tell-someone: "it's the site where the wildlife record of a real parcel gets stamped into a ledger as you scroll, and an endangered crane shows up in the data."
